The **Myrmidon** (Japanese: 剣士 _Sword Fighter_) is a sword-wielding class present in the [_Fire Emblem_ series](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire_Emblem_(series) "Fire Emblem (series)"). Their first appearance was in _[Fire Emblem Gaiden](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem\_Gaiden "Fire Emblem Gaiden")_. They appear in most game of the series. _[Fire Emblem Fates](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem\_Fates "Fire Emblem Fates")_ does not include myrmidons in core gameplay, instead featuring the functionally-identical [Samurai](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Samurai "Samurai") class; the Myrmidon class itself does, however, appear in the [DLC](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Downloadable_content_in_Fire_Emblem_Fates "Downloadable content in Fire Emblem Fates") chapter [Hidden Truths 1](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Hidden_Truths_1 "Hidden Truths 1") as the class used by [Owain](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Owain "Owain") in that chapter.

The class originated as a promotion of the [Mercenary](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Mercenary "Mercenary"), akin to the [Hero](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Hero "Hero") of most other games. In most games from _[The Binding Blade](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem:\_The\_Binding\_Blade "Fire Emblem: The Binding Blade")_ onward, the Myrmidon has been depicted as something of a counterpart to the Mercenary, with lower strength and defense in exchange for higher skill and speed.

Their initial strength and defense are lower than those of Mercenaries. To counter this, Myrmidons focus more in skill and speed, making critical hits their trademark; in some games, there are unique swords with higher critical rate (such as the [Wo Dao](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Wo_Dao "Wo Dao") and the [Shamshir](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Shamshir "Shamshir")) that can be wielded only by this class and its promotions.

Myrmidons, in most games, can be promoted to Swordmasters. Special cases are seen in _[Gaiden](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem:\_Gaiden "Fire Emblem: Gaiden")_, _[The Sacred Stones](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem:\_The\_Sacred\_Stones "Fire Emblem: The Sacred Stones")_, and _[Awakening](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem:\_Awakening "Fire Emblem: Awakening")_; in the former, myrmidons are a second-tier class and can be promoted to [Dread Fighters](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Dread_Fighter "Dread Fighter"), while in the latter two they can be promoted either to Swordmasters or [Assassins](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Assassin "Assassin").

In the [Elibe](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Elibe "Elibe") and [Magvel](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Magvel "Magvel") games, and _[Awakening](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem:\_Awakening "Fire Emblem: Awakening")_, Myrmidons were in general strongly associated with a particular region of the continents ([Sacae](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Sacae "Sacae"), [Jehanna](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Jehanna "Jehanna"), and [Chon'sin](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Chon%27sin "Chon'sin") respectively) and almost all units of the class are from these places. In other games, however, they can come from any land.
