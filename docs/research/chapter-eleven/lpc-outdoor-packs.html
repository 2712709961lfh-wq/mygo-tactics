[LPC] Outdoor Packs

Author: 
bluecarrot16
Friday, December 29, 2017 - 00:37
Favorites: 
22

Collection of packs for building 2D outdoor scenes using 32x32 tiles, in the style of the Liberated Pixel Cup. My goal has been to collect as many high quality assets from OGA and elsewhere as possible, and edit the color palette, perspective, style, etc. to fit the LPC style.

Future planned/in progress entries include: 

  • Rocks/boulders (coming soon)
  • Mountains/cliffs (coming soon)
  • Crops
  • Temple/ruins
