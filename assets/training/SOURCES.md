# Training Tower assets

- `slimes.png` is TinyWorlds’ **Slime** spritesheet, published under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The original sheet is bundled unchanged. The runtime selects the green and blue poses to distinguish the two sets of opponents. [OpenGameArt source](https://opengameart.org/content/slime-0)
- The tower exterior and the stone training floor are original procedural artwork in `src/training-art.js`.
