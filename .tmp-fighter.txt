The **Fighter** (Japanese: 戦士 _Fighter_) is a basic [class](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Class "Class") which debuted in _[Fire Emblem: Shadow Dragon & the Blade of Light](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem:\_Shadow\_Dragon\_%26\_the\_Blade\_of\_Light "Fire Emblem: Shadow Dragon & the Blade of Light")_ and is present in most titles in the [_Fire Emblem_ series](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire_Emblem_(series) "Fire Emblem (series)"). In almost every appearance it makes, it is a common infantry class which exclusively wields [axes](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Axe "Axe"). In _[Three Houses](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem:\_Three\_Houses "Fire Emblem: Three Houses")_, this class also specializes in [bows](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Bow "Bow") and [gauntlets](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Gauntlets "Gauntlets").

Conventional Fighters are defined by their immense [strength](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Strength "Strength"), also possessing significant [HP](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/HP "HP"). However, their other defensive stats are lacking in comparison to other classes (including a typically weak [resistance](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Resistance "Resistance") stat for a physical unit), usually causing them to be somewhat frail despite the relative longevity afforded by their sheer amount of HP. Furthermore, they typically have subpar [skill](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Skill_(stat) "Skill (stat)") and [speed](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"), which, combined with the low [hit](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Hit "Hit") and high [weight](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Weight_(weapon) "Weight (weapon)") of axes, limits Fighters' accuracy, evasiveness, and ability to follow up. Compared to the "criminal" axe-wielding [Pirate](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Pirate "Pirate"), [Brigand](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Brigand "Brigand"), [Barbarian](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Barbarian "Barbarian"), and [Bandit](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Bandit "Bandit"), Fighters often have lower HP and speed, but better skill, [luck](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Luck "Luck"), [defense](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Defense "Defense"), and resistance. In _[Fates](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem:\_Fates "Fire Emblem: Fates")_, Fighters possess high skill and speed, but have even lower defense and resistance; compared to the [Oni Savage](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Oni_Savage "Oni Savage") of Hoshido, Fighters are superior in strength, skill, speed, and luck, but inferior in magic, defense, and resistance.

In _Shadow Dragon & the Blade of Light_ and _[Mystery of the Emblem](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem:\_Mystery\_of\_the\_Emblem "Fire Emblem: Mystery of the Emblem")_, Fighters are incapable of promotion. _[The Binding Blade](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem:\_The\_Binding\_Blade "Fire Emblem: The Binding Blade")_ introduced the [Warrior](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Warrior "Warrior") as the _de facto_ promotion option of the Fighter class, which persisted throughout much of the rest of the series. In _[The Sacred Stones](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem:\_The\_Sacred\_Stones "Fire Emblem: The Sacred Stones")_ and _[Awakening](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem:\_Awakening "Fire Emblem: Awakening")_, which feature multiple class change options, Fighters have the choice of promoting to their normal advanced option of the Warrior, or the Mercenary's normal advanced option of the [Hero](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Hero "Hero"); additionally, in the former, the trainee [Journeyman](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Journeyman "Journeyman") class can promote to Fighter (or, alternately, to Pirate). In _Fates_, the [Berserker](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Berserker "Berserker") replaces the Warrior as one of the Fighter's promotion options.
