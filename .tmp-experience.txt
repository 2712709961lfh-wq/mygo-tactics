[Lyn](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Lyn "Lyn") has gained 10 experience points for attacking and damaging an enemy [Brigand](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Brigand "Brigand"). Killing the Brigand would have awarded her a much higher amount of experience.

**Experience** (Japanese: 経験値 _experience points_), abbreviated as **EXP**, **EX**, or **E**, is a value tied to a unit's growth and progression throughout the game. In every [_Fire Emblem_ series](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire_Emblem_(series) "Fire Emblem (series)") game, units earn experience by fighting, using [staves](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Staves "Staves") or performing other actions, and upon gaining enough experience points—100 in most games—a unit's [level](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Level "Level") will increase and they will [gain stats](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Growth_rate "Growth rate").

## Overview

Experience is amassed progressively throughout the game as the player's units take part in battles. By fighting and defeating enemies, or performing support acts like using [staves](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Staves "Staves"), [dancing](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Dance "Dance"), or [stealing](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Steal "Steal"), units are rewarded for performing these actions with small amounts of experience, directly tying a unit's growth to their active participation in the activities in battle.

Generally speaking, the amount of experience gained from any given battle is directly tied to how strong the allied unit is compared to the enemy they fight. A unit at level 1 in a [base class](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Category:Base_classes "Category:Base classes") will typically gain the highest possible amount of experience, with the amount awarded for defeating the same foe decreasing as they level up or [change class](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Class_change "Class change"). Conversely, higher-leveled and promoted enemies will award allied units progressively higher experience amounts; [bosses](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Boss "Boss") award the most significant amount of all, and for lower-leveled units defeating a boss will often award them a full 100 experience, enough to guarantee leveling up.

### Gameplay modes and experience

Several [gameplay modes](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Gameplay_modes "Gameplay modes") in certain _Fire Emblem_ games alter the experience growth of units, either as a side effect or as their primary point.

*   **_[Fire Emblem Gaiden](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem\_Gaiden "Fire Emblem Gaiden")_:** The "EASY" difficulty doubles all experience gain.
*   **_[Fire Emblem: Thracia 776](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem:\_Thracia\_776 "Fire Emblem: Thracia 776")_:** Paragon Mode doubles all experience gain. This stacks with the [Paragon](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Paragon "Paragon") skill.
*   **_[Fire Emblem: The Blazing Blade](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem:\_The\_Blazing\_Blade "Fire Emblem: The Blazing Blade")_ and _[Fire Emblem: The Sacred Stones](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem:\_The\_Sacred\_Stones "Fire Emblem: The Sacred Stones")_:** Normal mode increases experience gained from defeating an enemy.
*   **_[Fire Emblem: Path of Radiance](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem:\_Path\_of\_Radiance "Fire Emblem: Path of Radiance")_:** Easy Mode increases experience gained from defeating an enemy, while Difficult Mode reduces it, and Maniac Mode reduces it even further.
*   **_[Fire Emblem Awakening](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem\_Awakening "Fire Emblem Awakening")_:** The Lunatic and Lunatic+ difficulties, in addition to their changes to enemy stats, also lower experience growth in [SpotPass](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/SpotPass "SpotPass") skirmishes. Every attack in a SpotPass skirmish will award only a single experience point, even when enemies are killed. This does not affect actual chapters or [downloadable content](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Downloadable_content_in_Fire_Emblem_Awakening "Downloadable content in Fire Emblem Awakening") episodes.

### Bonus experience

_Main article:_[_Bonus experience_](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Bonus_experience "Bonus experience")
Exclusive to _[Fire Emblem: Path of Radiance](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem:\_Path\_of\_Radiance "Fire Emblem: Path of Radiance")_ and _[Radiant Dawn](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem:\_Radiant\_Dawn "Fire Emblem: Radiant Dawn")_ is **bonus experience** (often abbreviated as **BExp**), a system which rewards players for accomplishing certain goals with a supply of free experience. From the [base](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Base "Base") menu, the player can award any bonus experience points they've accumulated to their units to increase their experience. With every chapter cleared, the player will earn a minimum quantity of BExp, with additional BExp earned by quicker completion of the chapter or through accomplishing other tasks such as protecting NPCs.

In _Path of Radiance_, the [Paragon](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Paragon "Paragon") and [Blossom](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Blossom "Blossom") skills affect the amount of BExp required to level a given unit up;[[1]](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Experience#cite_note-SF9B-1) in _Radiant Dawn_, where the skills can be reassigned at any time, they do not.[[2]](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Experience#cite_note-PK10-2)

### Internal levels

As the [reclassing](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Reclass "Reclass") mechanism of _[Fire Emblem Awakening](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem\_Awakening "Fire Emblem Awakening")_ and _[Fire Emblem Engage](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem\_Engage "Fire Emblem Engage")_ theoretically allows for infinite experience and level gains, the game also implements a special checking mechanism to discourage excessive grinding by slowing experience gain after a certain point. This is accomplished through what are known as **internal levels**, a hidden value maintained for each character which keeps track of how many times they have reclassed and, upon reaching a certain internal level, penalizes their further experience gain. Internal level is defined using the formula below.[[3]](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Experience#cite_note-SF13-3)

Internal levels returned in _[Fire Emblem Fates](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem\_Fates "Fire Emblem Fates")_, modified not by reclassing but by promotion.

| Game | Formula | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| _[Awakening](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem:\_Awakening "Fire Emblem: Awakening")_ | Displayed [level](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Level "Level") + Promotion bonus + Cumulative level | Promotion bonus is 20 if the unit is in a promoted class, 0 otherwise. Cumulative level is initially 0. Each time the unit uses a [Second Seal](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Second_Seal "Second Seal"), [(Displayed level + Promotion bonus − 1) / 2] is added to this value. Cumulative level caps at 20 on Normal mode, 30 on Hard mode, and 50 on Lunatic(+) mode. |
| _[Fates](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem:\_Fates "Fire Emblem: Fates")_ | Displayed [level](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Level "Level") + Cumulative level | Cumulative level is initially 0. When a unit uses a [Master Seal](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Master_Seal "Master Seal"), it is increased by [(Displayed level + 20) / 2], rounded down. |
| _[Engage](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem:\_Engage "Fire Emblem: Engage")_ | Displayed [level](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Level "Level") + Cumulative level | Cumulative level is initially 0. When a unit uses a Master or Second Seal, it is increased by (displayed level - new level), where new level is the unit's level post-class change. Cumulative level caps at 30 on Normal mode, 40 on Hard mode, and 50 on Maddening mode. |
