The **Monk** (Japanese: 修道士 _Monk_) is a [basic](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Category:Base_classes "Category:Base classes") magical [class](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Class "Class") which debuted in _[Fire Emblem: The Blazing Blade](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem:\_The\_Blazing\_Blade "Fire Emblem: The Blazing Blade")_. In _The Blazing Blade_ and _[The Sacred Stones](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem:\_The\_Sacred\_Stones "Fire Emblem: The Sacred Stones")_, Monk is an offensive magical class which wields [light magic](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Light_magic "Light magic"), and is a male-exclusive class.[[1]](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Monk#cite_note-1) In _[Three Houses](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem:\_Three\_Houses "Fire Emblem: Three Houses")_ and _[Warriors: Three Hopes](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire\_Emblem:\_Warriors:\_Three\_Hopes "Fire Emblem: Warriors: Three Hopes")_, the Monk is an entry-level magic-user, able to wield both [black](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Black_magic "Black magic") and [white magic](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/White_magic "White magic"), and is not restricted by gender.

Compared to other offensive magic classes, the Monk possesses lower [magic](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Magic_(stat) "Magic (stat)"), but higher [skill](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Skill_(stat) "Skill (stat)") and [speed](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"); in conjunction with the typically lightweight and accurate [light magic](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Light_magic "Light magic"), Monks have excellent [accuracy](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Accuracy "Accuracy") and [attack speed](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Attack_speed "Attack speed").

In _The Blazing Blade_, Monks [promote](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Class_change "Class change") to the [Bishop](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Bishop "Bishop") class, gaining the use of [staves](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Staves "Staves"). In _The Sacred Stones_, Monks have the option to promote to Bishop or [Sage](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Sage "Sage").
